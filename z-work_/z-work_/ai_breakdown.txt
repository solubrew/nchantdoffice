The time it takes to change a tire can vary depending on several factors such as your experience, the tools you have available, and the conditions you're working in. However, for an average person with basic tools, the process typically takes between 20 to 30 minutes. Here’s a rough breakdown of the time required for each step:

### Estimated Time Breakdown

1. **Find a Safe Location and Prepare (3-5 minutes)**
   - Pull over safely, turn on hazard lights, apply the parking brake, and gather your tools.

2. **Loosen the Lug Nuts (2-3 minutes)**
   - Loosening the lug nuts before lifting the vehicle ensures they are easier to remove later.

3. **Lift the Vehicle with the Jack (3-5 minutes)**
   - Properly position the jack and raise the vehicle until the flat tire is off the ground.

4. **Remove the Flat Tire (2-3 minutes)**
   - Remove the loosened lug nuts and take off the flat tire.

5. **Mount the Spare Tire (2-3 minutes)**
   - Align the spare tire with the wheelbase and hand-tighten the lug nuts.

6. **Lower the Vehicle and Tighten Lug Nuts (3-5 minutes)**
   - Lower the vehicle so the spare tire touches the ground but doesn't bear the full weight, then tighten the lug nuts in a star pattern. Fully lower the vehicle and re-tighten the lug nuts.

7. **Stow Away Tools and Check Tire Pressure (3-5 minutes)**
   - Put away the jack, lug wrench, and flat tire. Check the spare tire's pressure if possible.

8. **Drive Carefully and Get Professional Assistance (Time Not Included)**
   - This step involves driving cautiously to a nearby repair shop to get the flat tire fixed or replaced.

### Total Estimated Time: 20-30 minutes

Keep in mind that these times are estimates and can vary. For example, if you have difficulty loosening the lug nuts, it might take longer. Conversely, if you’re experienced and have all the necessary tools readily available, you might complete the process more quickly.
