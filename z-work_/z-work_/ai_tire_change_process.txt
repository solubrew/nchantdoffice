Changing a tire is a relatively straightforward process, but it requires careful attention to safety and detail. Below is a step-by-step guide to changing a tire:

### Step-by-Step Guide to Changing a Tire

#### Tools and Materials Needed:
- Jack
- Lug wrench (tire iron)
- Spare tire
- Vehicle owner’s manual
- Wheel chocks (optional)
- Gloves (optional)
- Reflective vest and warning triangles (optional for roadside safety)

### Step 1: Find a Safe Location
- **Safety First:** If you’re on the road, pull over to a safe location away from traffic. Make sure the ground is level and firm.
- **Turn on Hazard Lights:** This will alert other drivers that you are stopped.
- **Apply Parking Brake:** Engage the parking brake to prevent the vehicle from rolling.
- **Use Wheel Chocks:** Place chocks or large stones behind the opposite tire to prevent the car from moving.

### Step 2: Prepare the Vehicle
- **Get Your Tools Ready:** Retrieve the jack, lug wrench, and spare tire from the trunk.
- **Loosen the Lug Nuts:** Use the lug wrench to loosen the lug nuts on the flat tire. Turn them counterclockwise. Do not remove them completely yet.

### Step 3: Lift the Vehicle with the Jack
- **Position the Jack:** Refer to the vehicle owner’s manual for the correct placement of the jack. It is usually under the vehicle’s frame near the flat tire.
- **Raise the Vehicle:** Turn the jack handle clockwise to lift the vehicle until the flat tire is about six inches off the ground.

### Step 4: Remove the Flat Tire
- **Remove the Lug Nuts:** Finish removing the lug nuts and set them aside in a safe place.
- **Take Off the Flat Tire:** Carefully pull the flat tire straight off the wheelbase.

### Step 5: Mount the Spare Tire
- **Align the Spare Tire:** Place the spare tire onto the wheelbase by aligning the holes with the lug bolt posts.
- **Hand-Tighten the Lug Nuts:** Put the lug nuts back on and hand-tighten them as much as possible.

### Step 6: Lower the Vehicle
- **Lower the Vehicle:** Use the jack to slowly lower the vehicle until the spare tire touches the ground but the full weight of the vehicle is not on the tire.
- **Tighten the Lug Nuts:** Use the lug wrench to tighten the lug nuts in a star pattern (tighten one, then the one opposite to it, and so on). This ensures even pressure on the wheel.

### Step 7: Fully Lower the Vehicle
- **Lower the Vehicle Completely:** Lower the vehicle all the way to the ground and remove the jack.
- **Check the Lug Nuts:** Tighten the lug nuts again to ensure they are secure.

### Step 8: Final Steps
- **Stow Away Tools:** Put the jack, lug wrench, and the flat tire back into the trunk.
- **Check Tire Pressure:** If you have a tire pressure gauge, check the pressure of the spare tire to ensure it is safe to drive on.
- **Drive Carefully:** Drive carefully and avoid high speeds. Spare tires are typically not designed for long-term use or high speeds.

### Step 9: Visit a Professional
- **Get the Flat Tire Repaired or Replaced:** As soon as possible, go to a tire repair shop to get the flat tire repaired or replaced and have the spare tire properly installed if it’s a full-sized spare.

By following these steps, you can safely and effectively change a tire. Always refer to your vehicle’s owner’s manual for specific instructions related to your vehicle, and prioritize safety throughout the process.
